FAQ

Do all users need the same plan?

No. Microsoft 365 business plans can be mixed in one company, for example Basic for staff who only need email and web apps, and a higher plan for those who need desktop apps or extra security.

Is there a user limit on business plans?

Microsoft's business plans are designed for smaller organizations and have a maximum number of users per plan. Check the current limit on Microsoft's site; larger organizations use enterprise plans.

Does the price include tax?

No. Sales tax depends on your state and is added at checkout.
