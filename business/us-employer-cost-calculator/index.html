How it works

Social Security = 6.2% × min(salary, wage base) Medicare = 1.45% × salary FUTA = FUTA rate × min(salary, $7,000) State UI = state rate × min(salary, state wage base) Total = salary + all of the above + other costs

Examples

$60,000 salary in 2026

Social Security $3,720 + Medicare $870 + FUTA $42 = $4,632 in federal employer taxes. If your state notice said 3% on the first $20,000 (example only), state unemployment adds $600, for a total of $65,232.

$200,000 salary in 2026

Social Security stops at the $184,500 wage base: 6.2% × 184,500 = $11,439. Medicare has no cap: 1.45% × 200,000 = $2,900. Plus FUTA $42 and your state amount.

FAQ

Is the employee's own 7.65% included?

No. The employee's Social Security and Medicare are withheld from their pay. This calculator shows only what the employer pays on top of salary.

What about the extra 0.9% Medicare tax on high earners?

That additional tax is paid by the employee, not matched by the employer, so it does not change the employer's cost.

Why does a Korean head office budget come out too low?

Budgets often use salary only. In the U.S., employer payroll taxes, benefits such as health insurance, and payroll service fees are added on top, so plan for the total shown here plus benefits.
