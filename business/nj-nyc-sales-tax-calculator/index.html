How it works

add tax: tax = price × rate total = price + tax remove tax: price = total ÷ (1 + rate) tax = total − price

Examples

$1,000 laptop bought in Manhattan

Tax = 1,000 × 0.08875 = $88.75. Total = $1,088.75.

Receipt total $53.31 in New Jersey

Price before tax = 53.31 ÷ 1.06625 = $50.00. Tax inside the total = $3.31.

FAQ

Is sales tax the same as VAT?

No. VAT is usually built into the shelf price and collected at each stage of the supply chain. U.S. sales tax is set by each state and local area, is normally added at the register, and is generally collected only on the final sale to the customer.

Does New Jersey have local sales taxes?

New Jersey uses a single statewide rate, according to the NJ Division of Taxation.

What about New York State outside NYC?

Counties and some cities add their own rate to the 4% state rate. Choose "Other rate" and enter the rate for that location from the New York State Department of Taxation and Finance.
