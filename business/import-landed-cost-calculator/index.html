How it works

duty = goods value × duty rate extra duty = goods value × additional rate MPF = goods value × 0.3464%, kept between $34.58 and $670.86 HMF = goods value × 0.125% (ocean only) landed = goods + freight + insurance + duty + extra + MPF + HMF + broker + other

The U.S. generally charges duty on the value of the goods, not on the international freight and insurance. Freight and insurance still count in your landed cost because you pay them.

Examples

$50,000 of goods by ocean, 2.5% duty

Duty $1,250 + MPF $173.20 + HMF $62.50 + freight $3,000 + insurance $150 + broker $250. Landed cost = $54,885.70, or $54.89 per unit for 1,000 units.

$5,000 of goods by air, duty free

0.3464% of $5,000 is $17.32, below the minimum, so MPF is $34.58. No HMF for air shipments.

FAQ

How do I find the duty rate?

Find your product's HTS code in the Harmonized Tariff Schedule at hts.usitc.gov, or ask your customs broker. The general rate is in the "General" column; extra tariffs may apply depending on the country of origin.

Is there a U.S. VAT on imports?

There is no federal VAT. Sales tax may apply later when goods are sold to the final customer, depending on the state.

Do small shipments pay these fees?

Low-value and informal entries follow different rules and fees. Check CBP's current guidance for your shipment.
