About this site

Business & IT Calculators is a personal project run by Steve Kim. It collects the calculations that small businesses in the U.S. keep needing for IT planning and day-to-day business decisions.

How the calculators are checked

  • Every formula is shown on its page, with worked examples you can check by hand.
  • Each calculator is tested against at least five hand-calculated cases before it is published.
  • Any price, tax rate or limit comes from an official source, shown on the page with the date it was last checked.

Limits

Results are estimates for planning. They are not tax, legal or financial advice. For decisions that matter, confirm with the vendor, your accountant or another qualified professional.

Your data

Calculations run in your browser. What you type is not sent to or stored on any server. See the privacy page.
